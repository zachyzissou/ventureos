# VentureOS Repo Charter

## Purpose
This repo is the **implementation home for VentureOS** — the operating system layer that turns OpenClaw into a multi‑business venture studio. It contains the **workflows, templates, and operational scaffolding** that make missions repeatable and safe.

VentureOS sits on top of the OpenClaw foundation:
- **OpenClaw foundation** = reliability, proactive engine, logging, and guardrails.
- **VentureOS** = business units, mission control, squads, and quality gates.

## Scope
**In scope**
- Mission Control (mission lifecycle, gates, artifacts)
- Business Unit registry + portfolio workflows
- Role cards, mission templates, and repeatable workflows
- Proactive scheduling + queue metadata for missions
- Ops scripts, runbooks, logs, and verification evidence

**Out of scope (for now)**
- Security hardening (deferred to later phase)
- Public publishing without explicit approval
- Irreversible actions without explicit approval

## Canonical Sources
- **Obsidian**: long‑horizon strategy, business unit notes, vision/roadmaps
- **This repo**: execution artifacts, runbooks, scripts, templates, and logs

## Working Rules
- Every non‑trivial change gets an issue/work item with evidence.
- Missions are not complete until their artifacts are linked and archived.
- Explicit approval is required for publishing, payments, destructive actions, and config changes.

## Naming
“VentureOS” is a working title. A naming sprint can happen once the roster and workflows are stable.
