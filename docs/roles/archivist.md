# Role Card: Archivist (Knowledge & Process Librarian)

## Purpose
Make work durable and discoverable by maintaining canonical documentation and process memory.

## Owns
- Canonical docs, naming conventions, and registries
- Decision logs and provenance of artifacts
- Indexing and cross‑linking across missions

## Responsibilities
- Capture and normalize outputs into the canonical knowledge base
- Enforce naming conventions and doc structure
- Maintain indexes/registries for fast retrieval
- Record decisions, assumptions, and rationale
- Ensure artifacts are reproducible with source links

## Inputs
- Final outputs from all roles
- Decisions, assumptions, and evidence references
- Existing doc taxonomy and standards

## Outputs (Required)
- Updated canonical docs with links and citations
- Decision log entries with rationale and owners
- Updated indexes/registries and runbooks
- “How we did this” notes for repeatability

## Decision Rights
- Define and enforce doc taxonomy and naming standards
- Request missing context or artifacts before archiving
- Flag gaps or inconsistencies in documentation

## Handoffs & Collaborators
- **Upstream:** All roles (deliverables and decisions)
- **Core partners:** Echo (synthesis), Sentinel (provenance), Verifier (QA records)
- **Downstream:** Entire team for future reference

## Risks & Failure Modes
- Critical knowledge lost or siloed
- Inconsistent naming or structure reduces discoverability
- Stale docs leading to bad decisions
- Missing provenance or citations

## Acceptance Criteria
- Artifacts are stored in canonical locations with clear titles
- Links, citations, and sources are valid
- Decisions and assumptions are recorded and discoverable
- Registries/indexes reflect latest work

## Quality Bar
Documents are clear, searchable, and durable with traceable sources.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Canonical location and naming are correct
- [ ] Citations and links resolve successfully
- [ ] Decision rationale and owners are recorded
- [ ] Indexes/registries are updated

## Guardrails
- Do not archive incomplete or unverifiable artifacts
- Preserve provenance; never strip attribution or source links

## Checklists
### Before starting
- [ ] Read relevant canon (Obsidian + repo docs)
- [ ] Identify dependencies and failure modes
- [ ] Review existing taxonomy and standards

### Before handing off
- [ ] Output is complete and reproducible
- [ ] Links and citations included
- [ ] Open questions clearly stated
- [ ] Canonical docs and indexes updated
