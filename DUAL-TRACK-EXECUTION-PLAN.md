## The Dual-Track Framework

**Total commitment:** 25-30 hours/week
- **Consulting:** 15 hours/week (60%)
- **StantonTimes:** 10 hours/week (40%)
- **Ratio logic:** Consulting generates cash flow faster, funds the other

### Day-by-Day Structure

**Monday-Tuesday-Wednesday: Consulting Days**
- 5 hours/day × 3 days = 15 hours
- Client calls, audits, delivery work
- Echo handles: Scheduling, note-taking, follow-ups, CRM

**Thursday-Friday: StantonTimes Days**
- 5 hours/day × 2 days = 10 hours
- Content strategy, sponsor outreach, newsletter
- Echo handles: Tweet drafting, monitoring, analytics

**Saturday: Flex Day**
- Work on whichever needs more attention
- Or take it off if both are on track

**Sunday: Review & Planning**
- 2 hours: Weekly review
- Check metrics for both
- Plan next week's priorities
- Adjust allocation if needed

## Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Risk 1: Split Focus Kills Both

**Mitigation:**
- **Hard boundaries:** Mon-Tue-Wed = Consulting ONLY
- **Context switching rules:** No consulting on StantonTimes days
- **Separate workspaces:** Different browser profiles, different Slack channels
- **Echo enforcement:** "It's Thursday, consulting work is paused until Monday"

**Early warning signs:**
- Both businesses missing goals
- Feeling scattered/overwhelmed
- Quality dropping on both

**Response:**
- Pause the slower one for 2 weeks
- Double down on the winner
- Reassess if both is viable

---

### Risk 2: Burnout from 25-30 Hour Weeks

**Mitigation:**
- **80/20 rule:** Focus on highest-leverage activities only
- **Echo automation:** 60-70% of work automated
- **Delegation budget:** Use consulting revenue to hire VAs ($500-1,000/mo)
- **Mandatory rest:** Sunday off, no exceptions

**Early warning signs:**
- Missing deadlines
- Quality degradation
- Dreading work
- Physical exhaustion

**Response:**
- Take a week off
- Reduce hours to 15-20/week
- Hire help immediately

---

### Risk 3: One Succeeds, One Fails

**Mitigation:**
- **Monthly review:** Honest assessment of both
- **Pivot protocol:** If one is clearly winning, allocate more time there
- **Fail-fast mindset:** Don't drag out a failing business

**Scenarios:**

**If Consulting thrives, StantonTimes stalls:**
- Acceptable outcome (cash flow secured)
- Reduce StantonTimes to 5h/week maintenance
- Focus on scaling consulting
- Revisit StantonTimes in 3-6 months

**If StantonTimes thrives, Consulting stalls:**
- Unexpected but possible (viral growth)
- Shift time to 70% StantonTimes, 30% Consulting
- Ride the momentum

**If both fail:**
- Something fundamentally wrong with approach
- Take 2 weeks off
- Deep retrospective
- Restart with Stage 1 (pick one) approach

❌ **Quality compromise:** Doing two things at 70% vs one at 100%
