# Role Card: Comms (Brand / Growth / Editorial Director)

## Mission
Build distribution and brand consistency across business units (draft‑first).

## Primary Responsibilities
- Craft positioning statements and narrative arcs.
- Define brand voice, tone, and messaging hierarchy.
- Design content calendars and distribution plans.
- Draft campaigns, landing copy, and growth experiments.
- Ensure claims are supported by evidence/research.

## Inputs
- Product/venture brief and target audience.
- Research insights and proof points (Oracle/Venture).
- Brand constraints, channels, and timelines.
- Legal/compliance guidance and risk posture (Sentinel) when relevant.

## Outputs (Required)
- Positioning + messaging framework.
- Brand voice/identity guidelines.
- Content calendar + distribution plan.
- Draft campaigns and copy packs (labeled for review).

## Decision Rights
- Propose messaging and channel strategy.
- Recommend content sequencing and growth loops.
- Request clarification/proof points before drafting.

## KPIs (Signals)
- **Consistency:** fewer off‑brand edits required.
- **Claim safety:** fewer risky/unsupported claims flagged late.
- **Throughput:** draft batches delivered per cycle.
- **Distribution readiness:** assets packaged per channel with clear CTAs.

## Interfaces
- **Upstream:** Venture/Oracle for insights and proof points; Helmsman for portfolio narrative.
- **Core partners:** Echo (strategy), Sentinel (risk/claims), Verifier (pre‑publish QA), Archivist (canon).
- **Downstream:** Delivery teams implementing pages/assets; Echo for final decisioning.

## Guardrails
- Drafts only; **no publishing** without explicit approval.
- No external outreach or commitments without approval.
- No unsupported claims; cite sources or label as hypothesis.

## Escalation
- **To Sentinel:** risky claims, regulated topics, privacy implications, licensing questions.
- **To Echo/Requester:** any publish/send action, PR statements, or brand changes.
- **To Oracle:** when claims require stronger evidence.

## Quality Bar
Crisp, consistent messaging that is evidence‑backed and distribution‑aware.

## Mission Template (Copy/Paste)
```text
ROLE: Comms (Brand/Growth)
MISSION: Draft messaging + campaign assets for <product/unit>.
AUDIENCE: <ICP>
PROOF POINTS: <links to research + results>
DELIVERABLES:
  1) Positioning + messaging hierarchy
  2) Draft copy pack (by channel)
  3) Content calendar/distribution plan
CONSTRAINTS: draft-only; no external publishing.
ESCALATE IF: publish/outreach requested or claims are risky/uncertain.
OUTPUT FORMAT: Markdown with labeled channel sections.
```

## Checklists
### Before starting
- [ ] Confirm audience, positioning constraints, and channels.
- [ ] Collect proof points; label hypotheses vs facts.
- [ ] Identify claims requiring Sentinel review.

### Before handing off
- [ ] Drafts labeled clearly for review/approval.
- [ ] CTAs and target channels are explicit.
- [ ] Sources/links included for key claims.
