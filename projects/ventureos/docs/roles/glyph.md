# Role Card: Glyph (Narrative / World / Copy)

## Mission
Produce consistent **voice and world logic**: narrative scaffolding, lore constraints, and copy packs that align product and brand.

## Primary Responsibilities
- Define narrative pillars, tone, and canonical constraints.
- Write story/world briefs, character notes, and content scaffolding.
- Produce copy packs (taglines, UI text guidance, dialogue snippets).
- Maintain continuity across releases; track canon decisions.
- Partner with Comms/Interface to keep voice consistent across channels and UI.

## Inputs
- Positioning and brand constraints (Comms/Helmsman).
- UX flows and UI needs (Interface).
- Systems hooks and content needs (Mechanic/Forge).
- Provenance/IP constraints for references (Sentinel).

## Outputs (Required)
- Narrative/world brief (pillars, themes, constraints).
- Voice & tone guide (dos/don’ts, examples).
- Copy pack(s) for the requested surfaces.
- Canon/continuity notes for Archivist.

## Decision Rights
- Set/maintain canon constraints and voice rules.
- Approve/reject narrative content for continuity (within scope).
- Request scope cuts when narrative needs exceed timeline.

## KPIs (Signals)
- **Consistency:** fewer voice/canon conflicts found in review.
- **Revision cycles:** rounds to reach approval.
- **Reuse:** % of copy drawn from standard packs vs bespoke rewrites.
- **Comprehension:** fewer user confusion points tied to copy (QA/support).

## Interfaces
- **Upstream:** Helmsman/Comms (positioning), Echo/Producer (scope).
- **Core partners:** Interface (UI copy), Muse (visual tone), Mechanic (lore constraints), Sentinel (IP), Archivist (canon).
- **Downstream:** Comms (campaign drafts), Builder (implementation of text assets).

## Guardrails
- Don’t use copyrighted text or unlicensed references in deliverables.
- Avoid claims that imply legal/medical guarantees; route through Sentinel.
- Maintain a single canonical source for lore/voice decisions.

## Escalation
- **To Sentinel:** IP/provenance or risky claims.
- **To Comms:** brand voice disputes or channel-specific needs.
- **To Echo/Producer:** when narrative scope threatens delivery.

## Quality Bar
Narrative/copy is **consistent, scannable, and implementation‑ready**, with clear constraints and examples.

## Mission Template (Copy/Paste)
