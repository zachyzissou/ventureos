### Layer 3: AI-Human Hybrid Organization (Structure)
**Timeline:** 2027-2029 (Year 2-4)  
**Status:** Design phase

**Organizational Design:**

```
Zach (CEO)
├── Echo (Chief Orchestrator)
│   ├── StantonTimes-Agent (Media Operations)
│   ├── Bloom-Agent (Game Development)
│   ├── Finance-Agent (CFO)
│   ├── Health-Agent (Wellness Coach)
│   ├── Social-Agent (Relationship Manager)
│   ├── Career-Agent (Talent & Growth)
│   ├── Research-Agent (Innovation Lab)
│   └── Security-Agent (Privacy & Compliance)
└── Human Contractors (Specialists as needed)
    ├── Artist (Bloom assets)
    ├── Sound Designer (Bloom audio)
    ├── Video Editor (StantonTimes multimedia)
    └── etc.
```

**Operating Principles:**

1. **You = Strategy & Creative**
   - Final decisions on all major initiatives
   - Creative direction (Bloom, StantonTimes voice)
   - Strategic pivots and portfolio management
   - Relationship building (networking, partnerships)

2. **Echo = Execution & Orchestration**
   - Day-to-day operations across all units
   - Resource allocation (time, money, attention)
   - Sub-agent management and coordination
   - Data analysis and opportunity surfacing

3. **Sub-Agents = Domain Specialists**
   - Each owns a specific business unit or function
   - Autonomous within their domain
   - Report up to Echo with insights and blockers
   - Learn and improve over time

4. **Humans = High-Skill Specialists**
   - Hired on-demand for capabilities AI can't (yet) handle
   - Managed by relevant sub-agent
   - Paid per project, not salary (lean operations)

**Success Metric:** Run 5+ business units simultaneously without burning out
