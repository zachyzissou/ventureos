## Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks
**Risk:** AI market shifts (new competitors, commoditization)  
**Mitigation:**
- Stay close to customers (feedback loops, advisory board)
- Invest in R&D (stay ahead on features)
- Build moat (community, integrations, research credibility)
- Diversify revenue (platform + business units + IP)

**Risk:** Economic downturn (customers cut budgets)  
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on ROI (prove value, justify cost)
- Flexible pricing (scale down options, not just up)
- Cash reserves (6-12 months runway)
- Portfolio diversification (B2B + B2C)

### Execution Risks
**Risk:** Scaling too fast (quality suffers, team breaks)  
**Mitigation:**
- Measure everything (NPS, churn, quality metrics)
- Hire slowly (bar stays high)
- Automate first, hire second (reduce dependency on humans)
- Stay lean (avoid fixed costs)

**Risk:** Burning out (too much on plate)  
**Mitigation:**
- Delegate aggressively (sub-agents + contractors)
- Say no often (focus on highest ROI activities)
- Health tracking (OpenClaw monitors stress, sleep, energy)
- Take breaks (quarterly sabbaticals if needed)

### Financial Risks
**Risk:** Cash flow issues (high investment, delayed revenue)  
**Mitigation:**
- Annual contracts (upfront payment)
- Credit line (backup, not primary funding)
- Reduce burn (cut non-essential expenses)
- Portfolio revenue (multiple streams, not dependent on one)

**Risk:** Customer concentration (one customer = 30%+ revenue)  
**Mitigation:**
- Diversify customer base (no one customer >10%)
- Expand market (more customers, less concentration)
- Long-term contracts (reduce churn risk)

### Personal Risks
**Risk:** Lose passion / interest (ADHD novelty seeking)  
**Mitigation:**
- Keep it interesting (new challenges, research, experiments)
- Delegate boring work (operations, admin)
- Explore expansions (new ventures, products)
- Exit if needed (no shame in selling and starting fresh)

**Risk:** Health issues (physical or mental)  
**Mitigation:**
- Proactive health management (regular check-ups, biometrics)
- Backup systems (can run without you for weeks)
- Insurance (health, disability, key person)
- Community support (advisors, friends, family)
