# AI Orchestration Consulting: Go-To-Market Strategy
*Actionable Implementation Playbook*

## Executive Summary

This document translates market research into executable actions for launching and scaling an AI orchestration consulting business. Built for solo consultants or small agencies targeting SMB operations leaders with **$5K-$75K project values** and **$2K-$5K/month retainers**.

**Core Strategy:** Position as the "done-for-you" automation partner for SMBs overwhelmed by AI tools and manual processes. Deliver fast wins (30-60 days), prove ROI, expand via retainers and referrals.

**First 90 Days Revenue Goal:** $15K-$30K (2-3 discovery assessments → 1-2 implementations)
**Year 1 Revenue Goal:** $150K-$250K (mix of projects + retainers)

## 1. POSITIONING & MESSAGING

### 1.1 Unique Value Proposition

**Primary Positioning Statement:**

> "We help overwhelmed operations teams turn 20 hours of weekly busywork into 5 minutes of automation — without hiring engineers or learning code. Done-for-you implementation. ROI in 60 days. Starting at $5K."

**Why This Works:**
- Specific outcome (20 hours → 5 minutes)
- Removes objections (no engineers, no code)
- De-risks (ROI promise)
- Clear pricing signal (not enterprise-only)

**Positioning Variations by Persona:**

**For Operations Leaders (Primary):**
> "Stop drowning in manual processes. We automate the workflows eating 20+ hours of your team's week—so you can scale without hiring. First wins in 30 days."

**For Technical Founders (Secondary):**
> "Focus on building product, not internal tools. We implement AI-powered automation so you can defer 2-3 hires and prove operational efficiency to investors. 6-week delivery."

**For Department Heads (Tertiary):**
> "Bypass the IT backlog. We deliver department-level automation that proves ROI in 60 days—then you scale it company-wide. Done-for-you, no IT dependency."

### 1.2 Differentiators (Competitive Moats)

**What Makes You Different:**

1. **Speed** - "60-day ROI, not 6-month projects"
   - Enterprise consultants take 6-12 months
   - You deliver in 6-8 weeks

2. **Done-For-You** - "We build it, not just tell you what to build"
   - Most consultants stop at strategy
   - You implement + train

3. **SMB-Focused Pricing** - "$5K-$25K, not $500K minimums"
   - Accessible to mid-market
   - Big 4 can't compete at this price

4. **Vendor-Neutral** - "Best tool for YOUR needs, not our partnerships"
   - Not locked into Zapier/Make/n8n
   - Platform-agnostic recommendations

5. **No Code Required** - "Your team manages it, no developers needed"
   - Knowledge transfer included
   - Sustainability post-engagement

**Proof Points to Develop:**
- Case study: "Saved [Company X] 15 hrs/week, $42K annually"
- ROI calculator: Interactive tool on website
- Video testimonials: 60-second client wins
- Before/after screenshots: Visual process transformation

### 1.3 Messaging Framework

**Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) Template:**

**PROBLEM:**
"You've adopted AI tools, but your team still spends 20 hours/week on copy-paste, data entry, and manual reporting. Tools don't talk to each other. Data lives in silos. Scaling means more hiring."

**AGITATE:**
"Meanwhile, you're paying $X,000/month in labor costs for work software should do automatically. Every week without automation costs you $Y. You've tried Zapier—it sat half-finished because nobody had time to figure it out."

**SOLVE:**
"We implement done-for-you AI workflow automation in 6-8 weeks. Your first process gets automated. Your team gets trained. You see ROI in 60 days. Then we help you scale it."

**Call-to-Action:**
"Book a free 30-minute audit. We'll identify your highest-ROI automation opportunity—no commitment required."

## 2. SERVICE PACKAGING & PRICING

### 2.1 Core Service Tiers

**TIER 1: AI READINESS AUDIT**

**What It Is:**
- 1-2 week discovery engagement
- Identify top 3-5 automation opportunities
- Prioritize by ROI
- Create 30/60/90-day roadmap

**Who It's For:**
- Companies unsure where to start
- Need to build internal business case
- Testing you before bigger commitment

**Deliverables:**
- 15-page assessment report
- ROI projections (hours saved × hourly cost)
- Prioritized automation roadmap
- 60-min presentation to stakeholders

**Pricing:** $1,500 (Starter) to $3,000 (Comprehensive)

**Profit Margin:** 80%+ (mostly your time + templates)

**Conversion Goal:** 70-80% buy Tier 2 within 30 days

**Positioning:**
- "Investment in clarity before spending $15K"
- "Risk-free way to see if automation makes sense"
- Credible to apply full fee toward Tier 2 if they proceed

---

**TIER 2: SINGLE WORKFLOW AUTOMATION** ⭐ (Most Popular)

**What It Is:**
- Implement 1 complete workflow automation
- 6-8 week timeline
- Includes training + 2 weeks post-launch support

**Who It's For:**
- SMBs ready to take action
- Clear pain point (reporting, data sync, lead routing, etc.)
- Budget-conscious but ROI-focused

**Deliverables:**
- Fully functional automation (live in production)
- Documentation (how it works, how to troubleshoot)
- Admin training (2-hour session)
- End-user training (1-hour session)
- 2 weeks of reactive support (bug fixes included)

**Pricing:** $8,000 (Simple) to $15,000 (Complex)

**Complexity Factors:**
- Simple: 2-3 tool integration, linear workflow (e.g., "New Typeform → Airtable → Slack")

**Profit Margin:** 60-70% (if productized well)

**Upsell Path:**
- 30-day check-in: "How's it working? Ready to automate workflow #2?"
- Retainer offer at 60 days

**Positioning:**
- "Your first automation pays for itself in 3-6 months"
- "Prove the ROI before scaling"
- "Most clients start here"

### 3.5 Conversion & Sales Process

**Discovery Call Structure (30 Minutes):**

**Minutes 0-5: Rapport**
- "Thanks for making time. How's your week going?"
- "What prompted you to reach out?"

**Minutes 5-15: Diagnosis (Ask Questions)**
- "Walk me through the process that's causing headaches."
- "How much time does your team spend on this weekly?"
- "What's that time worth? (Calculate: hrs × hourly rate)"
- "What have you tried already?"
- "What happens if you don't fix this in the next 6 months?"

**Minutes 15-25: Prescription (Your Solution)**
- "Here's what I'd recommend..."
- "We've done this exact automation for [similar company]—here's what happened..."
- "Timeline would be [6-8 weeks]. Investment around [$X-$Y range]."
- "ROI looks like [calculate based on their numbers]."

**Minutes 25-30: Close or Next Step**
- **If they're ready:** "Want to move forward? I'll send a proposal this afternoon."
- **If they need time:** "What questions do you have?" (address objections)
- **If they need buy-in:** "Who else needs to be involved? Let's schedule a call with them."
- **If not a fit:** "Honestly, I don't think we're the right fit because [reason]. Here's what I'd recommend instead..."

## 6. RISK MITIGATION

### 6.1 Common Failure Modes (And How to Avoid)

**❌ Failure Mode 1: "I'm too busy delivering to find new clients"**

**Prevention:**
- Block Mondays for sales (non-negotiable)
- Automate your own marketing (schedule LinkedIn posts in advance)
- Hire VA at $10K MRR to free up time

**❌ Failure Mode 2: "Clients don't see the value"**

**Prevention:**
- Over-communicate wins ("We saved you 8 hours this week!")
- Monthly ROI reports (quantify value)
- Set expectations low, over-deliver

**❌ Failure Mode 3: "Scope creep kills my margins"**

**Prevention:**
- Define scope tightly in proposal ("includes X, excludes Y")
- Change request process (anything outside scope = paid add-on)
- Track hours per project (if bleeding time, adjust pricing)

**❌ Failure Mode 4: "I'm building custom solutions every time"**

**Prevention:**
- Productize common workflows (templates)
- Say no to hyper-custom requests (or charge 2x)
- Document everything (build once, reuse forever)

**❌ Failure Mode 5: "Clients churn after 3 months"**

**Prevention:**
- Proactive outreach (don't wait for them to need you)
- Show ongoing value (monthly reports, new ideas)
- Lock in 6-month retainer minimums (with discount incentive)

---

### 6.2 Financial Safeguards

**Pricing Safety Nets:**
- Never start work without 50% deposit
- Retainers: Auto-billing, not manual invoicing
- Payment terms: Net 15 (not Net 30—cash flow matters)

**Diversification:**
- Target: No single client >30% of revenue
- Mix project + retainer revenue (predictable base)
- Multiple industries (recession-proof portfolio)

**Runway:**
- Maintain 3-6 months operating expenses in savings
- Track cash flow weekly (not just revenue)
- Reinvest profits slowly (don't overhire)

## 7. QUARTERLY REVIEW FRAMEWORK

**Every 90 Days, Ask:**

1. **Revenue:** Did we hit MRR + project targets?
   - If yes: What's working? Double down.
   - If no: Where did we fall short? Fix it.

2. **Pipeline:** Do we have 3x our quarterly target in pipeline?
   - If yes: Keep feeding top-of-funnel.
   - If no: Increase outreach/content volume.

3. **Delivery:** Are margins holding at 60%+?
   - If yes: Great, can we productize further?
   - If no: Where are we bleeding time? Fix or raise prices.

4. **Positioning:** Is messaging resonating?
   - Track: Discovery call → proposal conversion rate
   - If <50%: Messaging problem. Refine ICP or value prop.

5. **Moat:** What did we build this quarter that compounds?
   - Case studies, partnerships, content library, templates
   - Assets > activity
