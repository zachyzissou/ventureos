## Risk Mitigation

### Financial Risks
**Risk:** Revenue falls short of targets  
**Mitigation:**
- Diversified streams (not dependent on one source)
- Job as safety net (stable income while building)
- Low overhead (minimal fixed costs)
- Quick pivots (drop what doesn't work, double down on what does)

**Risk:** Over-investment in Bloom without revenue  
**Mitigation:**
- Budget cap at $10K (no scope creep)
- Contractor milestones (pay for delivered work only)
- Early playtesting (validate before polish)
- Phase 3 launch contingency (if not ready, delay vs. force)

### Time Risks
**Risk:** Can't balance job + side businesses  
**Mitigation:**
- Sub-agents handle execution (you handle strategy only)
- Batch work (consulting on weekends, Bloom evenings)
- Say no to distractions (focus on 3-4 core activities)
- Energy management (ADHD-friendly schedules, breaks)

**Risk:** Burnout from too much at once  
**Mitigation:**
- Weekly check-ins (Echo monitors stress signals)
- Drop lowest ROI activities if overwhelmed
- Adjust timelines (Phase 2 can extend if needed)
- Health tracking (sleep, exercise, downtime)

### Market Risks
**Risk:** Consulting market not ready for AI orchestration  
**Mitigation:**
- Start with warm network (lower education barrier)
- Free content builds authority (blog, Twitter)
- Pivot messaging (productivity, ADHD support, etc.)
- B2C and B2B options (individuals or small teams)

**Risk:** StantonTimes doesn't reach monetization threshold  
**Mitigation:**
- Growth hacking (engagement tactics, collaborations)
- Content experiments (find what resonates)
- Platform diversification (YouTube, podcast, Substack)
- Sponsorships as backup (don't need Twitter monetization)

**Risk:** Bloom launch delayed  
**Mitigation:**
- Flexible Phase 3 start (can slip to Q1 2027 if needed)
- Focus on consulting/StantonTimes revenue (de-risk Bloom)
- Early Access model (can launch imperfect and iterate)

### Execution Risks
**Risk:** Sub-agents not ready for autonomy  
**Mitigation:**
- Gradual handoff (increase autonomy over time)
- Clear escalation rules (when to ask for help)
- Weekly reviews (catch issues early)
- Rollback option (take back control if needed)

**Risk:** Legal/compliance issues  
**Mitigation:**
- Consult lawyer for contracts (one-time $500-1K cost)
- Liability insurance for consulting (E&O policy)
- Privacy compliance (GDPR, data handling)
- Tax professional (CPA for quarterly planning)
