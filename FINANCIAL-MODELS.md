# Assumptions & Methodology

## General Principles

**Conservative bias:** All revenue estimates skew pessimistic; costs skew high  
**Reality-based growth:** No hockey sticks; linear/gradual curves only  
**Hidden costs included:** Time, opportunity cost, mental overhead  
**Break-even focus:** Cash flow positive > vanity metrics

## Discount Factors Applied

- **Revenue timing:** Assume 30-day payment lag
- **Conversion rates:** Use bottom quartile industry benchmarks
- **Growth rates:** 50% of "typical" growth cited in case studies
- **Churn:** Assume 10% monthly for subscriptions
- **Seasonality:** Account for Q1/Q3 slowdowns
