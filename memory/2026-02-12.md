## Patio Heaters — Climate Entity Conversion

### Problem
- 4 patio heaters are 0-10V Control4 dimmers exposed as `light.*` entities in HA.
- HomeKit exposes them as lights → Siri "turn off all lights" kills the heaters.
- Needed a domain immune to both "turn on/off lights" AND "turn on/off fans" Siri group commands.

### Solution: Template Climate Entities
- Created 4 template climate entities mapping light brightness (0-255) to temperature (0-100°F).
- **Domain choice**: `climate` — immune to both light and fan Siri group commands.
- Temperature range 0-100 maps to brightness percentage (e.g., 75°F = 75% = 191 brightness).
- Modes: `heat` (on) and `off`.
