## Risk Mitigation

### Product/Market Risks
**Risk:** Bloom launch disappoints (low sales, bad reviews)  
**Mitigation:**
- Strong beta testing (caught issues early in Phase 2)
- Realistic expectations (Early Access = iterative improvement)
- Post-launch support (fix bugs fast, show commitment)
- Pivot if needed (lower price, bundle deals, regional discounts)

**Risk:** Multi-platform expansion dilutes brand  
**Mitigation:**
- Consistent branding across all platforms
- Repurpose content (maximize efficiency, minimize new creation)
- Data-driven decisions (double down on what works, cut what doesn't)
- Core platform remains Twitter (others are additive)

**Risk:** Course/community doesn't sell  
**Mitigation:**
- Presale validation (gauge interest before full production)
- Over-deliver on value (make it irresistible)
- Leverage existing clients (they're already fans)
- Refund guarantee (remove purchase friction)

### Execution Risks
**Risk:** Too many initiatives at once (burnout)  
**Mitigation:**
- Sub-agents handle execution (Zach = strategy + creative only)
- Track time per initiative (weekly review, adjust allocations)
- Cut lowest ROI activities if overwhelmed
- Phase 2 income provides cushion (less pressure)

**Risk:** Quality suffers from speed  
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on "good enough" vs. perfect (iterate based on feedback)
- Critical quality gates (Bloom must not ship broken, course must deliver value)
- User feedback loops (fix issues quickly)
- Reputation is long-term (don't sacrifice it for short-term gains)

### Financial Risks
**Risk:** Revenue shortfall vs. projections  
**Mitigation:**
- Diversified streams (not dependent on one source)
- Conservative projections (plan for lower end of range)
- Phase 2 income continues (buffer against shortfall)
- Quick pivots (double down on winners, cut losers)

**Risk:** Cash flow crunch (contractor payments, tool costs)  
**Mitigation:**
